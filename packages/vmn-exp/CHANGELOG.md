vmn-exp and vmn-exp-sdk share one version. Generated by git-cliff from the
commits under `packages/vmn-exp` and `packages/vmn-exp-sdk`; do not edit.
Every release is a 0.1.x patch, so what an operator or user has to do on
upgrade is in [upgrading.md](../../docs/vmn-exp/upgrading.md).
